Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

KING MOBILE SHOP & Electronic Centre (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website/app and how we use and protect it.

1. Information we collect

  • Name, mobile number and delivery address (house/street, landmark, village/city, district, state, PIN code);
  • Order details: items, quantity, price, payment mode, order notes and order history;
  • Basic technical data stored on your device (for example login details and recent orders saved in your browser/app storage).

We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, net-banking password or OTP.

2. How we use it

  • To accept, confirm, deliver and support your orders;
  • To contact you about your order by call, SMS or WhatsApp;
  • To process payments, refunds and prevent fraud or repeated fake orders;
  • To improve our shop, products and service.

3. Payments

Online payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment partner. Your payment details are entered on Razorpay’s secure checkout and are handled by Razorpay and your bank under their own privacy policies. We only receive the payment status and a payment reference ID.

4. Sharing of information

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with: our delivery staff (to deliver your order), our payment partner Razorpay (to process payment/refund), our cloud/spreadsheet service providers used to store orders (such as Google), and authorities when required by law.

5. Storage & security

Order data is stored in our business records and on your device. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no internet method is 100% secure. We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, warranty and legal purposes.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to view, correct or delete your personal information (subject to records we must legally retain) by contacting us. You can also clear the saved data from your browser/app settings.

7. Children

Our service is not meant for children under 18 to buy without a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will always be on this page with the updated date.
