Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

We deliver in and around our local service area. Delivery area, charges and timing for each order are confirmed by the shop when the order is accepted.

  • Processing time: orders are usually confirmed within a few hours during shop timings.
  • Delivery time: generally same day to 3 working days after confirmation, depending on your location and product availability.
  • Delivery charges: if any, are told by the shop before dispatch – “to be decided at shop” is shown at checkout.
  • Pickup: you can also collect the product from our shop.
  • Tracking: order status (Placed, Confirmed, Out for Delivery, Delivered) is visible in the My Orders section, and our delivery person will call you before delivery.
  • Address: please give a complete address with landmark and a 6-digit PIN code and keep your phone reachable. We are not responsible for delays caused by wrong or incomplete addresses.
  • Open the parcel carefully: please check the product at the time of delivery and report any damage within 48 hours as per our Refund & Return Policy.
