This policy applies to all products ordered from KING MOBILE SHOP & Electronic Centre through our website/app, whether paid online or by Cash on Delivery.

1. What can be returned / refunded

Returns, replacements and refunds are accepted only for defective products, that is:

  • Product is dead on arrival (DOA) or not working as described;
  • Product received is damaged or broken in transit;
  • Wrong product / wrong model delivered compared to what was ordered.

We do not accept returns or refunds for change of mind, wrong selection by the customer, product not liked, or price differences.

2. How to report a defect

  • Report within 48 hours of delivery by calling/WhatsApp on 9558222519.
  • Share your Order Number, name, mobile number, a clear photo/video of the defect, and (if possible) an unboxing video.
  • Our team will verify the issue and confirm within 1–2 working days.

3. Conditions

  • Product must be in original condition with box, accessories, warranty card and bill.
  • Defect must not be caused by physical damage, liquid/water damage, burn, tampering, misuse or unauthorised repair after delivery.
  • Serial number / IMEI (where applicable) must match our sales record.
  • Recharge, vouchers and other digital items are non-refundable once delivered/activated.

4. Resolution

For a verified defect we will, at our discretion, first offer a replacement or repair. If a replacement is not possible, a refund will be issued. Defects found after 48 hours are handled under the brand/shop warranty mentioned on the product page (if any).

5. Refund process & timeline

  • Once the returned product is received and approved, the refund is initiated within 2 working days.
  • Online payments (UPI / card / net banking via Razorpay): refunded to the original payment method. It usually reflects in 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Cash on Delivery orders: refunded by bank transfer / UPI to the account you provide, or in cash at the shop.

6. Order cancellation

  • You may cancel an order before it is dispatched by calling/WhatsApp us. Online-paid orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in 5–7 working days.
  • Orders that are refused at the doorstep without a valid defect, or repeatedly booked and cancelled, may lead to the customer’s ID being blocked from placing new orders.
  • We may cancel an order if the product is out of stock, the price was shown incorrectly, or the address cannot be served. In such a case you get a full refund.

7. Failed or double payments

If money is deducted but your order is not confirmed, or you were charged twice, the amount is automatically reversed by the payment partner/bank within 5–7 working days. You can also contact us with your Razorpay Payment ID for help.
